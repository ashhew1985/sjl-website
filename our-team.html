01 / About the business

Industry experience.
One dedicated team.

Steven Johnson Limited is a specialist consultancy working with Independent Connection Providers (ICPs), Independent Distribution Network Operators (IDNOs) and businesses operating within the UK's electrical infrastructure sector.

The team is headed by founder and director Steve Johnson. Experience represented within SJL includes electricity network engineering, project delivery, safety management, technical audits and senior utility auditing experience with Lloyd's Register Quality Assurance (now LRQA), with a particular focus on NERS.

One point of contact.
Support from start to finish.

We aim to take much of the pressure out of accreditation: supplying appropriate document suites, preparing for assessment, identifying compliance gaps and continuing to support organisations after accreditation.

Rather than separate, disconnected services, we offer joined-up support tailored to each client's operational scope and priorities.

02 / What we bring

Technical knowledge.
Practical solutions.

Our approach is informed by experience on sites, within management teams and through independent utility auditing. We understand the systems and evidence expected in an accreditation assessment, alongside the realities of operating and maintaining electricity infrastructure.

We support ICPs with NERS applications, full technical and compliance document suites, gap analysis and assessment preparation. For IDNOs, our focus extends to embedded network asset compliance, site audits, contractor assessment, personnel authorisations and ongoing technical support.

  • Scope-specific documentation for NERS, Achilles and Constructionline requirements
  • Competency assessments, contractor audits and compliance reviews
  • Embedded network inspection and asset assurance advice
  • Authorising Engineer consultancy subject to agreed responsibilities

05 / How we work

A comprehensive service.
An ongoing relationship.

For ICPs, we can take on much of the work involved in preparing for independent NERS assessment, including documentation, gap analysis and corrective-action support.

For IDNOs, we provide asset-focused technical assurance, contractor auditing, inspection support and specialist advice. In both sectors, our role can continue through ongoing reviews, document updates and competency assessments.

Practical support, tailored to you.

We agree the services that fit your organisation's activities, responsibilities and accreditation scopes.

Formal accreditation awards remain the responsibility of the independent accreditation body; inspection and authorisation responsibilities depend on the agreed appointment and scope.
