SJL works with electricity connection providers and network owners to take the complexity out of accreditation and ongoing compliance. FOR ICPs

Accreditation from start to finish

A one-stop source of support for NERS applications, scope selection, complete document suites, pre-accreditation gap analysis, competency assessments and preparation for independent audit — followed by ongoing compliance support.

Independent Connection Providers

NERS accreditation.
Without the unnecessary complexity.

From initial application through to preparation for independent assessment and beyond, SJL can take on much of the documentation and preparation work, helping your team concentrate on running its business.

01

Application & scope planning

Support with identifying applicable NERS scopes, preparing your application and understanding the requirements relevant to your activities.

02

Complete document suites

Supply of tailored, compliant procedures, processes and supporting documentation for ICP and IDNO activities, including requirements associated with NERS, Achilles and Constructionline where applicable.

03

Gap analysis & pre-accreditation audits

Identification of gaps in management systems, processes and procedures against relevant scheme and accreditation requirements.

04

Competency assessment & corrective actions

Technical competency interviews, preparation for assessment and practical assistance with addressing findings and non-conformances.

05

Support through independent assessment

Preparation and practical guidance through the external assessment process. Accreditation decisions and awards remain with the relevant independent accreditation body.

06

Continued compliance support

Ongoing internal and external audit preparation, document updates, competency reviews, personnel authorisation support and technical advice.

Independent Distribution Network Operators

Focused support for
asset compliance.

Practical asset-focused consultancy for owners and operators of embedded electricity networks. Our work can include condition checks, photographic inspection evidence, reporting on identified concerns and advice on appropriate follow-up.

01

Asset compliance

Visual checks and compliance reviews of embedded network assets, including condition, security, warning notices, identification and other agreed inspection points.

02

Inspections & technical audits

Structured inspections and technical audits of embedded network assets, with documented findings, photographic records and recommendations.

03

Key-holder & personnel management

Advice on access arrangements, key-holder controls, staff competence, assessment and personnel authorisation procedures.

04

Maintenance, repair & remedial works

Technical advisory support for asset maintenance, repairs and remedial works, with any physical delivery subject to a separately agreed scope.

Ongoing independent support

Contractor assurance.
People, processes & performance.

Helping ICPs and IDNOs establish and maintain confidence that work carried out by their own teams and external contractors meets applicable technical and scheme requirements.

01

Contractor assessment & audits

Assessment and ongoing compliance auditing of contractors working on your behalf, including relevant technical, safety, procedural and scheme requirements.

02

Compliance & technical site audits

Independent site-based reviews, inspection processes and audit findings with practical recommendations for corrective action.

03

IDNO-trained Authorising Engineer support

Specialist Authorising Engineer consultancy for companies and personnel, including support with competence assessment, safety-rule arrangements and authorisation processes, subject to the agreed appointment and scope.

04

Ongoing technical support

Continued audits, updates to procedures and documentation, staff assessments and technical advice as operational requirements evolve.
