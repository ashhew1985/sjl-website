Who we support

Industry experience.
Practical guidance.

Every network business operates differently. SJL works with ICPs and IDNOs to understand their operational requirements and provide focused technical and compliance advice, particularly around substations and associated processes.

ICP

Independent Connection Providers

Support for businesses delivering electrical connections, including technical procedures, competency records, accreditation preparation and substation-related compliance.

IDNO

Independent Distribution Network Operators

Advisory support for electricity distribution operations, including documentation, inspection processes, personnel authorisations and ongoing technical governance.
