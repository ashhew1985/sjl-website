NERS support

Prepare with confidence for independent accreditation assessment.

SJL helps organisations understand and prepare the technical evidence and management documentation relevant to their NERS accreditation scope.

  • Reviewing required procedures and supporting documentation.
  • Technical competency interviews and personnel assessments.
  • Pre-accreditation audits identifying potential gaps.
  • Advisory support to address findings ahead of assessment.
  • Ongoing assistance with compliance documentation.
SJL provides consultancy and preparation support; accreditation decisions are made by the relevant independent accreditation body.
