01 / Steve's story

Experience earned in the field.
A business built around people.

Steve Johnson's career has taken him from engineering and electrical infrastructure delivery into senior project management, safety leadership and utility auditing. That broad, practical background gives him an understanding of the work behind the paperwork — and the pressures faced by the people responsible for delivering it.

After working as a Senior Utility Auditor with Lloyd's Register Quality Assurance, focusing primarily on NERS, Steve established Steven Johnson Limited in 2020. He believed the sector needed a more personal, joined-up service — one that brought expertise often spread across several large organisations together under a single, accessible consultancy.

One point of contact.
A clearer route forward.

Steve's objective is to help clients feel supported, prioritised and confident in their next steps — not passed between multiple service providers.

Seeing that original vision become a working business remains the achievement Steve is most proud of.

02 / Specialist expertise

A practical understanding of
electrical infrastructure.

Substations are Steve's particular specialism. His experience across site delivery, engineering, management, safety and auditing helps him see both the operational reality and the compliance requirements of an organisation.

Today, SJL primarily works with Independent Connection Providers (ICPs) and Independent Distribution Network Operators (IDNOs), supporting businesses with NERS preparation, technical competency, pre-accreditation audits, documentation and corrective-action advice.

  • Grounded in site and project experience
  • Strong understanding of NERS audit requirements
  • Practical, cost-conscious routes to improvement
  • Safety considered throughout the process

05 / Looking ahead

Building a consultancy
with a future.

Steve's ambition goes beyond growing the company's client base. He wants to develop a business structure that can bring younger people into the industry, offer practical training and provide clear goals for career progression.

As SJL grows, the aim is to pass on knowledge gained over decades while maintaining the personal, cost-effective service on which the business was founded.

Keeping clients at the centre.

Steve most enjoys helping customers find clear, workable and cost-conscious solutions while supporting high safety standards across the industry.

The goal is not to complicate compliance. It's to make the route forward more understandable and achievable.
