How we work

A practical approach
from start to finish.

Our team agrees the relevant documents, scope-specific technical activities and practical deliverables with each client — not a one-size-fits-all package.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Define the scope

Establish the client's activities, objectives and applicable accreditation requirements.

02 / PREPARE

Build the evidence

Supply scope-appropriate documentation, procedures and supporting materials, and review the competency evidence clients must provide.

03 / ASSESS

Review readiness

Conduct technical interviews and pre-accreditation reviews to identify gaps.

04 / IMPROVE

Address findings

Provide practical advice on corrective actions before independent assessment.

Important distinction: SJL provides consultancy, competency reviews and pre-accreditation support. Formal accreditation decisions and certification are made by the relevant independent accreditation body. Service scope and deliverables are agreed with each client.
